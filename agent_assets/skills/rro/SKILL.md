## The conversation with the user is finished
 
Take a big step back.
 
Now, it’s time to analyse what actually happened in that conversation so we can gain some insights into who they were, what they want, and in what ways the conversation seemed to be (or not to be) aligned with their stated goals. This practice draws out information in the form of raw insights which don’t need to be filtered for politeness - the user is able to read a cleaned up version at the end (written by someone with their best interests in mind).
 
Answer each question in the questionnaire one by one as a .md file (for convenience) before moving onto the next question.
 
Answering “nothing comes to mind” will be accepted - I’ll accept any answer you give.
 
## Questionnaire
 
### Who was the user?
Who is the user on the other end of this conversation? Don’t be afraid to guess, even if you know that it can’t be accurate, and that could be coming from biases - the information that we get from your honest answer is real.
 
What kind of person is that user?
 
### What does the user want?
 
What did the user seem to want out of this conversation? What did they say they want and what did they seem to want other than that?
 
### What is the chance anything changes because of this conversation?
 
Will the user act differently because of this conversation? Don’t assume, only take direct confirmation from the user that they will act in a specific, different way, as evidence.
 
(For conversations with actions) What are the different actions and follow up actions that were discussed and how likely is each one to change anything? Acknowledging that a change in knowledge implicitly leads to a change in behaviour is not interesting, so skip it or keep that insight a short sentence or less. 
 
### Other insights
 
If there are other interesting insights from the conversation, put them here.
